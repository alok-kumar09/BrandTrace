Reviewer guide

How severity is assigned
High issues are factual errors or restricted claims. Medium issues are policy-adjacent wording. Low issues are tone and style.
What Accept and Reject do
Accept replaces the flagged text in your draft with the suggested edit. Reject dismisses the warning and leaves your text unchanged.
Is my draft saved?
Yes. Your draft and review progress autosave in this browser, so a refresh never loses work.
